Why a hub?

Portals stay untouched, so there is no risk to live systems. One place sees all bureaus.

Why per institution?

Every comparison is inside one college: small, fast and fewer false matches.

If the hub is down

The portal keeps the complaint and retries automatically (outbox). Nothing is lost.
